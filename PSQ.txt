I. Purpose Statement

The Project Status Questionnaire (PSQ) is the standard periodic health check for any active Regent project. It is not a progress report to an outside party. It is a self-examination: a structured way for the Prime, a collaborator, or an agent to take stock of where a project is, what is working, what is blocked, and what is needed to move forward.

The PSQ is completed at regular intervals during a project's life:
- At the start of a new working session on a project that has been dormant for two or more weeks
- At the close of a major phase or milestone
- When a project feels stuck, unclear, or in need of redirection
- On request by the Prime

The PSQ does not make decisions. It surfaces information so that decisions can be made.

________________

II. Usage Rules

1. Complete one PSQ per project per status cycle.
2. The PSQ may be completed by the Prime, by an agent on the Prime's behalf, or collaboratively.
3. Agent-completed PSQs must be flagged for Prime review.
4. A PSQ that reveals a material change in project scope, direction, or viability should trigger a PCM (Project Change Memo).
5. No field is left blank. Use N/A, None, or [Unknown] where the value is not known or does not apply.
6. Completed PSQs are archived in the project folder:
   Regentorium / Core Projects / [Project Name] / Status
   Naming convention: PSQ_YYYY-MM-DD_[ProjectHandle].txt

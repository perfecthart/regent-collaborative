I. Purpose Statement

The Archive Overview (AO) is the canonical map of Regentorium — The Regent Collaborative Archive. It exists to give any agent, collaborator, or the Prime a complete and authoritative picture of what the archive contains, how it is organized, what the disposition of each major artifact is, and how the governing framework relates to the archive's physical structure.

The AO does not govern. It describes, registers, and orients. Authority remains with TUOP, REG-COLLAB, and the Constants. The AO is the navigator; the Constants are the law.

The AO is updated whenever:
- A new project is added to the archive
- A document's disposition changes
- A folder is created, renamed, or restructured
- A governing document is added or retired
- The Prime issues a change directive affecting archive structure

III. Disposition System

All documents and projects in the archive carry one of four dispositions. No field is left blank — use N/A where a disposition does not apply.

Disposition Vocabulary:

  RETAIN-Active
  The document or project is alive, in use, and under active development or regular consultation.

  RETAIN-Archive
  The document or project is complete, stable, or historical. It is preserved in place. Not under active development but not flagged for removal.

  FLAGGED-Suggest Delete
  The document or project has been identified as a candidate for removal, consolidation, or deprecation. It may not be deleted without Prime approval. The flag is advisory only.

  PRIME DECISION-Pending
  A decision about this document or project is awaiting Prime review and directive. No action is taken until the Prime resolves it.

Rules:
- Only the Prime may authorize deletion or permanent disposition change.
- A disposition of FLAGGED-Suggest Delete does not authorize removal; it initiates a review cycle.
- Agents may flag but may not delete.
- All flags must include a rationale note.
